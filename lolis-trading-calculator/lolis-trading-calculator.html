Reference

What the extension means

< 50%Weak / Failed Extension

Price breaks out of the initial range by less than half of the IB height and falls back inside. This shows that buyers or sellers are defending the boundary, signaling a Range Bound or Neutral Day suited for mean-reversion trades back toward the IB midpoint or opposite end.

50% – 100%Moderate Trend / Normal Day

Price extends beyond the range by half to one full length of the IB height. A pullback after this move typically offers a trend-continuation entry, such as retesting the broken Initial Balance Low from below as new resistance.

> 100%Trend Day / Double Distribution

Price moves more than 1.0x the total IB height past the initial boundary. This signals strong institutional conviction, leading to shallow pullbacks and a sustained directional Trend Day.
